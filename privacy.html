Privacy Policy

Only what it needs to alert you

No sign-up, no name, no email address, no location, no ads, no tracking. Effective November 11, 2026.

No account

The first time you open Tee Time Alerts, our server gives the app a random private key, which the app keeps in your iPhone's Keychain. That key is what ties your watches to you. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

What our server keeps

  • Your watches: the courses, days, time windows, number of players and holes you choose, so the server can check the tee sheets for you.
  • Your phone's push-notification token, issued by Apple, so alerts reach your phone.
  • Alert history and status: which open tee times were found and alerted, so you aren't alerted twice for the same time.
  • Your subscription status: its expiry date and Apple's transaction ID, which the app and Apple send to the server so it knows to check your watches. Apple handles all payments; we never see your payment details or Apple Account.
  • A course booking login, only if you add one: the username and password for the course's booking site, stored encrypted and used only to sign in to that site for you, to show resident tee times and your booked rounds. Removing the login in the app's Settings deletes it.

Who else is involved

  • Apple delivers push notifications and handles App Store purchases and subscriptions.
  • The courses' booking site (foreUP): our server reads public tee sheets there, and signs in with your login only if you added one.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather forecasts. The app asks for the forecast at a course's location; your own location is never used or sent.

Like any web server, ours keeps short technical logs of requests, which can include your IP address, for troubleshooting. They are rotated away automatically.

Our server is hosted by Hetzner. We don't sell or share your information, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Keeping and deleting it

Watches stay until you delete them or they expire. Deleting a watch in the app removes it from our server. To delete everything we hold for you, email us from the address below and we'll erase it.

Children

Tee Time Alerts is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
