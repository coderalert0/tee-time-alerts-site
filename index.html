What it does

Watches

Pick one or more courses, the days, a time window, how many players, and 9 or 18 holes. Repeat it every week if you like.

Instant alerts

A push notification the moment a matching tee time opens. Tap it to go straight to the course's booking page.

Open Times

Every open tee time across the courses for the next few weeks, with the weather. Free for everyone.

Booked

Connect your own course booking login to see resident tee times and your upcoming rounds.

Courses

Tee Time Alerts currently works only with San Diego's public golf courses: Torrey Pines South, Torrey Pines North, Balboa Park 18, Balboa Park 9 and Mission Bay. More may come later.

Price

Open Times is free. Watches and alerts need Tee Time Alerts Pro: $9.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

FAQ

Why didn't I get an alert?

Check that notifications are on for Tee Alerts (Settings > Notifications), and send yourself a test from the app's Settings. Alerts come only for times inside your watch's window, for the number of players you chose, while your subscription or free trial is active. Popular times can be booked within seconds of opening, so tap the alert right away.

Which courses does it cover?

Only San Diego's public golf courses for now: Torrey Pines South and North, Balboa Park 18 and 9, and Mission Bay.

Does it book the tee time for me?

No. Tapping Book opens the course's own booking page at that tee time, and you finish booking there.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions > Tee Time Alerts Pro. You can also open it from the app's Settings with Manage Subscription.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting a watch removes it from our server, and removing a booking login in Settings deletes that login. To delete everything, email us and we'll erase it. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this the official city or course app?

No. Tee Time Alerts is independent and isn't affiliated with the City of San Diego, the golf courses, or their booking provider.
